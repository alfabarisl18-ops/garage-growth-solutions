Privacy notice

Your inquiry stays focused on your request.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Information collected

When you request a free audit, the form asks for your shop name, your name, website or Google Profile URL, email address, phone number, primary concern, and any optional notes you provide.

How it is used

Alpha Barrie uses this information to review your request, prepare or discuss the audit, respond to you, and determine whether the offered service is a fit. It is not sold or used to create unrelated mailing lists.

Form processing

The contact form is processed by Formspree. Submitting the form sends the information to that service so it can deliver the request. Do not submit passwords, payment details, customer records, or other sensitive information through the website form.

Retention and requests

Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, or provide an agreed service. You may reply to any message you receive from Alpha Barrie to request correction or deletion of your inquiry information, subject to records that must be retained for an active client relationship.

Demo website

The Bay & Beacon Auto Care demo form does not transmit or store information. It exists only to demonstrate the user experience of an auto-shop appointment form.
