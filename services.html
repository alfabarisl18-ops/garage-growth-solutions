Before we scope it

Website and growth questions.

Are the published prices fixed?

No. They are introductory starting prices. Your written proposal confirms the final price after page count, content, platform, integrations, timing, and migration needs are understood.

What costs are separate from the website price?

Domains, paid platforms, advanced booking or payment tools, e-commerce, large migrations, extra pages, professional photography, and other approved third-party charges are separate.

Who owns the website and accounts?

The client retains ownership of their domain and business accounts. Final client-specific website files and approved assets transfer after full payment, subject to any separately licensed platform or third-party materials.

How many revisions are included?

One-page websites and individual pages include one consolidated revision round. Multi-page websites and redesigns include two consolidated revision rounds. New direction or added scope is quoted separately.

Can you guarantee rankings, calls, or booked work?

No. The work can improve technical foundations, clarity, relevance, trust, and the contact path, but no provider controls Google, customer decisions, or business results.

Can I start with the free audit instead?

Yes. If you are unsure what you need, start on the Audit & Trust page and receive the three most important findings first.
