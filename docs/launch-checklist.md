### Monday through Friday

- [ ] Research and contact 10 new shops.
- [ ] Complete five scheduled follow-ups.
- [ ] Update every outcome and next action in the private tracker.

### Every week

- [ ] Visit three to five selected local shops.
- [ ] Deliver three personalized free audits.

## Scaling gates

### First client

- [ ] Record time spent on every delivery step.
- [ ] Fix unclear instructions and remove unnecessary work.
- [ ] Turn repeated work into a checklist or reusable template.

### $1,000 per month

- [ ] Aim for four $247 care clients, supported by setup projects.
- [ ] Keep one standard audit, onboarding, delivery, and reporting method.
- [ ] Review which outreach source produces qualified conversations.

### $2,000 per month

- [ ] Raise new setup work to the established $497 price.
- [ ] Set a maximum number of active delivery projects.
- [ ] Batch Google updates and monthly reports.

### $5,000 per month

- [ ] Add higher-value website projects with separate scopes and deposits.
- [ ] Introduce a premium growth tier only after the $247 care workflow is stable.
- [ ] Stop accepting custom low-value tasks that do not support the core outcome.

### $10,000 per month

- [ ] Document every repeatable operating procedure.
- [ ] Delegate production tasks with clear quality checks.
- [ ] Keep account ownership, approvals, strategy, and client communication under direct control.
- [ ] Use capacity limits and a waiting list instead of lowering delivery quality.
