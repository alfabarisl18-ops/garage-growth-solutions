# Client delivery kit

## One-page offer sheet

### Local Trust & Calls Setup

**Founding-shop price:** $297 for the first three completed clients. Standard price afterward: $497.

**Payment:** 50% before work begins; 50% at delivery.

**Target:** seven business days after deposit, intake, and required access are received.

### Included

- **Google Business Profile audit and owner-approved cleanup:** review categories, services, hours, contact details, description, photos, and other customer-facing information; document priorities; apply only approved safe changes.
- **Website trust and conversion review:** review the homepage and main contact path on phone and desktop for clarity, trust signals, calls to action, contact details, working links and forms, and mobile usability; deliver prioritized recommendations. Website editing is separate.
- **Homepage copy pack:** write a ready-to-use main headline, supporting paragraph, primary button wording, short trust points, and closing call to action. Website implementation is separate.
- **Review-request toolkit:** provide a direct Google review link, QR code, two text-message templates, and one email template. Automated sending is separate.
- **Print-ready review counter card:** provide the digital print file with the review QR code. Physical printing is separate.
- **Business information consistency check:** compare core business details on the website and Google Business Profile and document mismatches. Mass directory cleanup is separate.
- **Before-and-after summary and 30-minute handoff:** document findings, completed changes, delivered files, boundaries, and next steps, then walk through them with the client.
- **One revision round and one 30-day check-in:** accept one consolidated, in-scope revision list and later confirm that delivered links and tools still work.

### Not included

Full website development, paid advertising, ongoing SEO, photography, suspended-profile reinstatement, unlimited revisions, and guarantees of rankings, calls, leads, bookings, or revenue.

## Website testimonial publishing workflow

- Ask every eligible customer for honest feedback through the shop's direct Google review link; do not filter the request based on expected sentiment.
- The shop owner must approve any real testimonial selected for the website and confirm that the wording and customer attribution are accurate.
- Add approved testimonials manually during a separately scoped website project or an eligible Client Care content update.
- Never automatically publish an open-form submission, offer an incentive for a positive review, or promise a particular rating or review volume.
- Keep fictional demo testimonials labeled as samples and never reuse them as client proof.
