# Bay State Auto Growth services catalog

This page records the public service structure and delivery boundaries used by the Websites & Growth page. Final client proposals must still define the exact scope, payment schedule, timing, platform, content responsibilities, and approved third-party costs.

## Website projects

### One-page conversion website — from $997

- one conversion-focused page;
- strategy and page structure;
- essential copy support;
- mobile-first design;
- click-to-call and inquiry form;
- basic on-page and technical SEO;
- accessibility and browser checks;
- one consolidated revision round;
- launch checklist and handoff.

### Multi-page website — from $1,797

- up to five core pages built from scratch;
- conversion-focused page structure and essential copy support;
- mobile-first design, calls to action, and inquiry path;
- basic on-page and technical SEO;
- accessibility and browser checks;
- two consolidated revision rounds;
- launch checklist and handoff.

### Existing-site redesign — from $1,497

- redesign of up to five existing pages;
- content cleanup and migration review;
- mobile, trust, and contact-path improvements;
- basic on-page and technical SEO;
- accessibility and browser checks;
- two consolidated revision rounds;
- launch checklist and handoff.

### Service or location page — from $297

- one clearly scoped service or legitimate location page;
- search-friendly structure and relevant call to action;
- one consolidated revision round.

Multi-location work begins with the applicable website package plus each additional page. E-commerce, payments, advanced booking, large migrations, custom integrations, domains, paid platforms, extra pages, photography, and third-party services require separate approval and pricing.

## Ongoing and specialist services

### Ongoing local SEO — from $497 per month

Month-to-month support for one shop location. At least three months is recommended because search improvements take time.

- website and Google Business Profile improvements;
- local content and page optimization;
- Search Console and performance monitoring where access is available;
- plain-language monthly summary.

### Google Ads — consultation and custom quote

- account and campaign readiness review;
- budget, destination-page, and conversion-tracking assessment;
- campaign plan and management only after written approval;
- ad spend paid directly by the client through a client-owned account.

### Photography planning and coordination — from $297 plus partner fee

- shop-specific shot list;
- scheduling and photographer handoff coordination;
- image selection and website-ready optimization support;
- photography partner's fee approved separately.

### Suspended-profile appeal support — from $297

- issue and policy review;
- document-readiness checklist;
- one appeal-preparation cycle;
- one follow-up after Google's response.

Clients submit official evidence through the appropriate Google process and should not send registration documents through the public Bay State forms. Google controls all appeal outcomes.

### Client Care Plan — $247 per month

Available after an eligible completed project. The existing scope in `client-delivery-kit.md` remains authoritative.

## Standing boundaries

- no unlimited revisions;
- no guarantees of rankings, calls, leads, bookings, revenue, advertising performance, review volume, or platform approval;
- no third-party purchase or advertising spend without client approval;
- clients retain ownership of their domains and business accounts;
- final client-specific files transfer after full payment, subject to separately licensed platform or third-party materials.
