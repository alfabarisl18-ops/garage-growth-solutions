Clear scope

Exactly what you receive.

No vague marketing language. These are the specific reviews, written materials, and tools included in the Local Trust & Calls Setup.

01

Google Business Profile audit & approved cleanup

I review the shop’s categories, services, hours, contact details, business description, photos, and other customer-facing information. You receive a prioritized correction list, and I make only the safe changes you approve.

Does not include suspended-profile reinstatement, ownership disputes, or ranking guarantees.
02

Website trust & conversion review

I inspect the homepage and main contact path on phone and desktop through a customer’s eyes. I check whether services are clear, proof and contact details are easy to find, buttons guide people to act, and important links or forms work.

You receive prioritized recommendations. Redesigning or editing the website is quoted separately.
03

Ready-to-use homepage copy pack

I write a stronger main headline, supporting paragraph, primary button wording, short trust points, and a closing call to action. The copy is delivered in a clean document so the shop or its website provider can place it on the homepage.

This is writing, not a new website or code implementation. One revision round is included.
04

Review-request toolkit

You receive a direct Google review link, a scannable QR code, two ready-to-send text messages, and one email template. The wording makes it easier to ask real customers for honest feedback after a completed job.

Automated sending, incentives, review filtering, and guaranteed review volume are not included.
05

Print-ready review counter card

I prepare a digital counter-card file with the review QR code and a simple customer instruction. The shop can print it and place it where customers pay or pick up their keys.

The print-ready file is included; physical printing and display hardware are separate.
06

Business information consistency check

I compare the shop name, phone, address, hours, website, and key service information shown on its website and Google Business Profile. Any mismatch is documented so customers are not given conflicting information.

This is a focused check, not a mass directory-listing or citation-cleanup campaign.
07

Before-and-after summary & handoff

You receive a clear record of what was found, what changed, which files and links were delivered, and what should happen next. We then walk through it together during a 30-minute handoff.

You keep the final client-specific copy and files after full payment.
08

One revision & 30-day check-in

You can send one consolidated list of in-scope revisions after delivery. About 30 days later, we check that the delivered links and review tools still work and discuss any sensible next step.

New pages, major rewrites, and ongoing monthly work require a separate scope.

Common questions

Straight answers before you commit.

What is included in the free audit?

You receive a one-page review with three priority findings and a personalized three-to-five-minute screen recording. There is no obligation to buy.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or a number of leads?

No. Rankings, calls, and bookings depend on factors no honest provider controls. The work improves clarity, trust, and the path customers use to contact you.

What is a homepage copy pack?

It is ready-to-use writing for the main page of your existing website: a stronger headline, supporting paragraph, primary button wording, short trust points, and a closing call to action. It does not include website design or coding.

What is a website trust review?

It is a customer-eye review of the homepage and main contact path on phone and desktop. I check clarity, trust signals, service information, calls to action, contact details, links, forms, and mobile usability, then give you prioritized recommendations.

Does the $297 setup include a new website?

No. The setup includes the homepage copy pack and website trust review described above. Website design or implementation is quoted separately after the audit.

Will I keep control of my accounts?

Yes. You remain the owner. Access should be granted through available manager or collaborator roles instead of sharing passwords.

What happens after the setup?

You can take the completed system and run it yourself. If ongoing support makes sense, a month-to-month care option is discussed during the handoff.
